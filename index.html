Knowledge Centre

Farmer Certification Guide (NPOP)

  • Choose a Certification Body: Pick from APEDA's accredited list (e.g., ECOCERT, Bureau Veritas).
  • Registration: Submit farm history, land maps, and Aadhar/PAN to the CB.
  • TraceNet Registration: Get registered on APEDA's TraceNet 2.0 system.
  • Conversion Period: 2-3 years of organic practice required before label usage.
  • Inspection & Audit: Annual site inspection for compliance.

Organic Fertilizer Recipe

Jeevamrut

Mix 10kg cow dung, 5-10L cow urine, 2kg jaggery, 2kg pulse flour, and a handful of forest soil in 200L water. Ferment for 48-72 hours.

Panchagavya

A potent mixture of 5 cow products: milk, curd, ghee, urine, and dung, fermented over 21 days.
