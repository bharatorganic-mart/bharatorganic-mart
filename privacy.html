Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Bharat Organic Mart ("Platform", "we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Platform. By using the Platform, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us and information that is automatically collected when you use the Platform.

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Registration: Name, email address, phone number, password (hashed), account type (Individual Farmer, FPO, ICS Group, Processor/Manufacturer, Supplier/Trader, Buyer, Importer/Exporter), business/organization name.
  • Profile Information: Business/organization name, contact person, address (city, state, postal code), website, business description, products/services offered, production capacity, export markets.
  • Certification Documents: Organic certification certificates, certificate numbers, certification body names, scope of certification, validity periods, certificate copies (uploaded voluntarily).
  • Listings and Requirements: Product/service details, descriptions, quantities, pricing, certification details, origin, location, images, specifications.
  • Enquiries and Communications: Messages sent through the Platform, enquiry details, quotations, responses, contact preferences.
  • Payment and Transaction Data: (If applicable) Transaction history, payment method details (processed by third-party providers, not stored by us).

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, time spent, clicks, search queries, filters applied.
  • Device and Browser Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device type, screen resolution, referrer URL.
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: Session cookies, authentication tokens, preference cookies, analytics cookies (see Section 11).
  • Log Data: Access timestamps, request/response metadata, error logs, security events.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and Operate the Platform: Create and manage your account, authenticate you, provide Platform features, process listings and enquiries.
  • Facilitate Connections: Match buyers with sellers, display listings and requirements, enable enquiries and quotations.
  • Communicate with You: Send account notifications, security alerts, platform updates, enquiry notifications, marketing communications (with consent).
  • Verification and Compliance: Verify identity, business registration, and organic certification; comply with legal obligations.
  • Improve the Platform: Analyze usage patterns, fix bugs, develop new features, personalize experience.
  • Security and Fraud Prevention: Detect fraud, unauthorized access, spam, abuse; enforce Terms of Service.
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with applicable laws, respond to legal requests, enforce Terms of Service.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as described below:

  • With Other Users (Platform Functionality): Your business name, contact person, location, listings, and requirements are visible to other Users as part of Platform functionality (enquiries, listings, requirements). Certification details you choose to display are visible to other Users.
  • With Service Providers: We may share data with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf (hosting, analytics, email delivery, authentication, payment processing). They are contractually obligated to protect your data.
  • For Legal and Safety Reasons: We may disclose information when required by law, court order, government request, or to protect rights, property, or safety of Users or the public.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of the business.
  • With Your Consent: We may share information for marketing or other purposes with your explicit consent.

We do not sell personal data to third parties for their marketing purposes.

5. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

5.1 Data Storage

Data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure (Supabase/PostgreSQL) with servers located in [Region to be confirmed]. Backups are encrypted and stored securely.

5.2 Security Measures

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Row Level Security (RLS) policies on database tables
  • Supabase authentication with JWT tokens, secure password hashing (bcrypt)
  • Regular security monitoring, vulnerability scanning, and penetration testing
  • Access controls: principle of least privilege, role-based access

5.3 Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained while account is active; deleted within 30 days of account deletion request.
  • Transaction/enquiry records: Retained for 7 years for legal/compliance purposes.
  • Analytics/log data: Retained for 24 months (aggregated/anonymized where possible).
  • Certification documents: Retained while account is active; deleted on account deletion.

6. Your Rights (Under Applicable Data Protection Laws)

Depending on your location and applicable law (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in India), you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.
  • Objection: Object to processing for direct marketing or legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for processing based on consent (where applicable).
  • Complaint: Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (e.g., Data Protection Board of India).

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@bharatorganicmart.com. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law. Identity verification may be required.

6. Certification Documents and Sensitive Data

Organic certification documents (certificates, scopes, validity periods, certificate copies) are treated as sensitive business information. We:

  • Store certificates securely with encryption at rest
  • Display certification details only as you choose (per Listing/profile settings)
  • Do not share certificate copies with other Users without your consent
  • Use certificate data only for verification and Platform functionality
  • Delete certificates upon your request or account deletion

We do not share certification documents with third parties except as required for Platform functionality (e.g., verification with certification bodies) or by law.

7. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside India where our service providers operate. We ensure appropriate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy decisions, or other lawful mechanisms) for such transfers in compliance with applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child, we will delete it promptly.

9. Third-Party Links

The Platform may contain links to third-party websites (certification bodies, government portals, logistics providers). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

10.1 Types of Cookies

  • Essential Cookies: Required for authentication, session management, security, and core Platform functionality. Cannot be disabled.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your preferences (language, region, display settings).
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand usage patterns (pages visited, time spent, features used) to improve the Platform.
  • Marketing Cookies: (If enabled) Used to deliver relevant communications about Platform features and updates.

10.2 Cookie Management

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may impair Platform functionality. Analytics and marketing cookies can be declined via our cookie banner (when implemented) or browser settings.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. Material changes will be communicated via email or Platform notification at least 30 days before taking effect. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated.
